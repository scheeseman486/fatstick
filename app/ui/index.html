~~~ --- TRK

Open a playlist, pick a stick, copy, then lock the order.

No playlist

M3U, M3U8, XSPF (VLC) and PLS playlists work.

    Open a playlist to start, or drop the file on this window.

    Drag tracks to change the order, or select one and press Alt+↑ / Alt+↓.

    USB stick

    Leave empty to use the top level of the stick.

    1. Copy the files

      One at a time, through your system.

    2. Set the order at FAT level

      Rewrites the folder's entry table, then reads it back from the stick and checks every track. Your system asks for your password or fingerprint.

    3. Unplug

      The stick stays unmounted, so nothing changes it after the check.
