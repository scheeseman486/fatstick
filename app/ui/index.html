~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ NO PLAYLIST
~~~ --- TRK

Open a playlist, pick a stick, then erase and write.

No playlist

M3U, M3U8, XSPF (VLC) and PLS playlists work.

    Open a playlist to start, or drop the file on this window.

    Drag tracks to change the order, or select one and press Alt+↑ / Alt+↓.

    USB stick

    Leave empty to put the tracks at the top level of the stick.

    Writing erases the whole stick

    Everything on it is replaced by a fresh FAT32 file system holding only this playlist. Copy off anything you want to keep first.

    1. Erase and write

      One fresh FAT32 partition, laid out here: tracks in playlist order, each file in one unbroken block, back to back. Your system asks for your password or fingerprint once.

    2. Read it all back

      The partition, every table and every byte are checked against your files.

    3. Unplug

      The stick is ejected, so nothing changes it after the check.
